About Me

Erika Costecalde isa 21-year-old web design student with a passion for creating visually thoughtful digital experiences. She enjoys combining creativity with problem-solving to turn ideas into designs that are both functional and intuitive. She considers herself curious and, direct. She enjoy asking questions, and exploring new ideas. Her straightforward nature helps her approach challenges honestly and focus on finding effective solutions. She believes good design is about more than aesthetics. It should communicate clearly, serve a purpose, and create a positive experience for the people who interact with it. Outside of her studies, she enjoys a variety of creative and active pursuits. Swimming and hiking allow her to stay active and spend time outdoors, while cooking and embroidery help to unleash her creative side.
